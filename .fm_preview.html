Function Interpretation

Summary

The module is dominated by glutamine, glutamate and aspartate together with several nucleotide phosphates. Their coordinated increase is consistent with anaplerotic entry into the TCA cycle feeding de novo pyrimidine synthesis rather than with a general amino-acid transport change.
